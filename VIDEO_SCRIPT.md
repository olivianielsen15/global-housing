# Video Presentation Script - Global Housing Data Visualization
**Duration:** ~50-55 seconds | **Tone:** Informal, conversational

---

## Script

**[0:00 - Opening Hook]**
"Want to understand the global housing crisis? I built an interactive 3D globe that lets you explore housing data across 91 countries."

**[0:07 - Quick Demo]**
*[Rotate globe, click a country]*
"You can spin the globe, click any country, and instantly see metrics like housing affordability, mortgage debt, construction activity—even natural disaster risk."

**[0:17 - Key Features]**
*[Switch between layers]*
"Switch between 10 different data layers. See where housing is most unaffordable—spoiler: it's not where you think. Or find the biggest investment opportunities for affordable housing development."

**[0:27 - Data Sources]**
"All the data comes from trusted sources: OECD, World Bank, UN-Habitat. No guesswork—just real numbers that policymakers and researchers actually use."

**[0:37 - The Why]**
"I created this because housing data is usually buried in spreadsheets. This makes it visual, interactive, and actually useful for anyone trying to understand global housing markets."

**[0:47 - Call to Action]**
*[Show URL on screen]*
"Check it out at globalhousingdatabase.com—it's free, works on mobile, and you can use it for research, policy work, or just exploring. Link in the description!"

**[0:55 - End]**

---

## Presentation Tips

### Visual Cues to Match Script:
1. **Opening (0:00-0:07):** Show the full globe rotating slowly
2. **Demo (0:07-0:17):** Click Switzerland or another country, show stats panel
3. **Layers (0:17-0:27):** Switch between 2-3 layers (Price-to-Income → Investment Opportunity → Disaster Risk)
4. **Sources (0:27-0:37):** Scroll to show data sources section briefly
5. **Why (0:37-0:47):** Show the globe from different angles, demonstrate smooth interaction
6. **CTA (0:47-0:55):** Show URL clearly on screen, maybe zoom into a specific interesting country

### Delivery Notes:
- **Speak at normal pace** - Not too fast, but energetic
- **Emphasize numbers** - "91 countries," "10 layers," "trusted sources"
- **Sound excited but not salesy** - You built something useful, share that energy
- **Pause briefly** after "spoiler: it's not where you think" for effect

## Background Music Suggestions:
- **Upbeat but subtle** - Don't overpower your voice
- **Instrumental** - No lyrics
- **Modern/Tech vibe** - Something that matches data visualization energy
- Suggested: Artlist "Positive Technology" or Epidemic Sound "Minimal Tech"

## Recording Setup:
- **Screen recording:** Use OBS or Loom to capture browser
- **Voiceover:** Record separately for better quality, then sync
- **Or:** Record while presenting if you prefer natural flow
- **Export:** 1080p minimum, 60fps for smooth globe rotation
